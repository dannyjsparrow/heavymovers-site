The job

“Just a few boxes,” said every client ever

You're the new guy on the crew. Joey texts you the job every morning. Then the client opens the door, and the "small move" is a piano, a second floor nobody mentioned, and a kitchen nobody packed.

  • 3,000 lb

    Every piece weighs what it weighs

    That's a small move. Books drag, dressers sway, and the couch needs a buddy on the other end and a pivot at every door.

  • 1–4

    Bring your crew

    Co-op online with a join code. Or go solo and the guys grab the other end for you.

  • Truck Tetris

    Heavy to the cab, tiers wall to wall, strap it all down. Then drive it. The truck leans and brakes long, and a sloppy load lets you know.

  • Something always goes wrong

    The couch sticks in the door. The ex shows up for the lamp. The dog runs off with a sock. The client grabs the other end and walks the wrong way.

  • Roast them back

    Roast whoever you're looking at and they fire right back. Fill the meter and the whole crew goes turbo.

  • Start your own

    Your name, colours and emblem on the shirts and the truck. Weekly contracts against other crews.

  • Level up

    Lifting, driving and stamina grow as you work. Hit the gym, get a haircut. The crew notices.

  • Joey texts

    Some nights: "need a favour. tonight. 3am. its an easy one. cash." Don't open the crate.
